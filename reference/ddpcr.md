# Digital droplet PCR measurements and metadata from a study of people living with cystic fibrosis

Digital droplet PCR measurements and metadata from a study of people
living with cystic fibrosis

## Usage

``` r
ddPCR
```

## Format

A data frame object with 74 observations of 4 covariates.

- Value:

  droplet digital PCR, giving the observed total bacterial load in the
  sample in copies/uL

- Treatment:

  1 if the sample is from a subject receiving antibiotics, 0 otherwise

- SpecimenType:

  the body site of origin of the sample, either saliva or sputum

- Subject:

  an ID identifying the participant

## References

Lu, J et al. (2020). *Parallel Analysis of Cystic Fibrosis Sputum and
Saliva Reveals Overlapping Communities and an Opportunity for Sample
Decontamination*. ASA Journals mSystems, 5, 4. \<doi:
10.1128/msystems.00296-20\>.
